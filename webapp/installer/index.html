HORTATOR LOOM

Install Hortator LOOM on your FM-1

A fork of Hortator, the drum-machine firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1, with one addition: LOOM, a colour theme in the black, white and per-track colours of LOOM-8 (MENU > COLOR). Everything else is Hortator 1.0.1. It installs over USB from this page, in a few minutes.

  1. Use Chrome or Edge

  2. Connect the FM-1

  3. Install

This build has not been tested on an FM-1. Its screens were checked in the simulator only.

It installs over Hortator and keeps its projects, presets and settings. An FM-1 that already has Hortator keeps the colour it had: choose LOOM in MENU > COLOR (hold HOME).

Recovery guide. FM-1-transporter
